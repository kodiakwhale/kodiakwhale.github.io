starboard /ˈstärˌbôrd/
a multiplayer pirate survival game, featuring:
  • Shipbuilding: Place your own cannons, storage, etc. anywhere on board
  • Base Building: Build a castle to protect your treasure
  • Sieges: Raid other players' bases with trebuchets
  • Open World: Explore a vast archipelago on 100+ player servers
